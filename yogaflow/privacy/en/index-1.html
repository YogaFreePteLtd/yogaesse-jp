This Privacy Policy explains how Yoga Free Pte. Ltd. ("Yogaesse," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares personal data when you use the Yogaesse Yoga Flow app and related services (the "Platform"). It applies to app users and to anyone who contacts us (for example, by email). It does not cover data we collect from our own employees or contractors, or information that has been fully anonymized.

1. Who is responsible for your personal data

Yoga Free Pte. Ltd., a company incorporated in Singapore, is the data controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at admin@yogaesse.com.

2. What personal data we collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect:

  • Account information — email address, and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the identifiers those services provide.
  • Subscription and purchase status — whether you have an active subscription and which plan, provided to us by the app store platform and our subscription-management provider; we do not receive or store your card number or other payment credentials.
  • Usage information — which videos or programs you view, playback activity, and general app interaction, used to power features like recommendations and to understand how the app is used.
  • Device and technical information — device type, operating system version, app version, and similar diagnostic information, collected automatically.
  • Information you provide to us directly — for example, if you email us with a question or support request.

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the Platform is not directed at children.

3. Why and how we use your personal data

We use personal data to: provide and maintain the Platform (including verifying your subscription so you can access content); operate core features such as recommendations; respond to your support requests; maintain the security and integrity of the Platform; understand usage patterns so we can improve the app; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases for processing (where applicable)

Where required by law (for example, under the GDPR for users in the EEA/UK), we rely on one or more of the following bases: performance of our contract with you (providing the subscription service you signed up for), our legitimate interests (improving and securing the Platform), compliance with a legal obligation, and, where applicable, your consent.

5. How we protect your data

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved to protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

6. Where we store and process data

Personal data may be processed and stored by us and by our service providers in Japan, Singapore, and other countries where our infrastructure or vendors operate. Where data is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure it continues to be protected consistently with this policy.

7. Who we share your data with

We share personal data with:

  • App store platforms (Apple, Google) — to process your purchase and verify your subscription status;
  • Service providers who help us run the Platform, including subscription and entitlement management, cloud database hosting, video hosting and delivery, and product-analytics providers, each acting under contractual obligations to protect your data and use it only to provide services to us;
  • Legal and regulatory bodies — where required to comply with a valid legal request from a court, law enforcement, tax authority, or other authorized government body.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@yogaesse.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law. You may also manage or cancel your subscription, and delete your account, directly from your device's app store account settings or by contacting us.

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the law, our business, or our practices. Updates take effect once posted, and we will update the "Last Updated" date above.
