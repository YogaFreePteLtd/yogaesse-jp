About the subscription

Yogaesse is subscription-only.

  1. Yearly plan: billed once per year
  2. Monthly plan: billed each month, with a free trial for new subscribers

Pricing is shown in the app and varies by region and currency. Either plan unlocks every lesson in the app; there are no one-off, per-video purchases.

Restoring a purchase

If you've switched devices or reinstalled the app, use "Restore Purchases" in the app to recover your existing subscription. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you originally subscribed with.

Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel any time from your device: Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Yogaesse. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period.

A video won't play

Check your internet connection and try restarting the app. If that doesn't help, email us at admin@yogaesse.com with your device model and iOS version.

Refunds

Refunds for purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
